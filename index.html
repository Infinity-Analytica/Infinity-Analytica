Monthly VAT Verification System

Audit-ready VAT validation designed to protect input claims and withstand SARS scrutiny.

Businesses lose billions annually due to fraudulent or invalid VAT registrations.

Supplier VAT statuses can change without notice, exposing businesses to disallowed claims and audit adjustments. Monthly verification creates a dated, defensible record that strengthens compliance and ensures every claim can be substantiated.

  • Protect input VAT claims with verified supplier status
  • Reduce audit exposure and risk of SARS reversals
  • Maintain dated, defensible compliance records
  • Enable accounting firms to deliver audit-ready client files
VAT Act Section 16(2) Input tax may only be deducted if supported by a valid tax invoice.
VAT Act Section 20 A tax invoice must reflect accurate supplier details, including VAT registration status.
SARS Audit Practice Vendors must retain sufficient records to substantiate VAT claims during audit.
